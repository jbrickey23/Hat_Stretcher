# Hat_Stretcher

Durable engineering project for controlled stretching of damp hat felt over a crown block without marring usable brim.

## Status — reconciled 2026-10-21
**Lead gripper: MyLifeUNIT wide-jaw canvas stretching pliers.** User reports strong effective grip and no observed marring in initial test. Real blocking session and sustained marks/fatigue/ergonomics evaluation remain open. Vise-Grip with flat PLA inserts is a functional backup; nose-biased force distribution and attachment geometry remain unresolved. A 1/8-inch flat wood test insert also worked preliminarily. The earlier tarp clips slipped while locked and are rejected as-is.

**Priority:** test canvas pliers on a complete blocking session, without modifications; then decide whether spring/flip latch or controlled lever is warranted. No new lever design is approved. The user has explicitly preferred the simplest workable solution.

## Established setup
- Millinery Warehouse medium-weight felt; #51 block, 5.75-inch target crown.
- Approximately 5 inches of material below break cord is usable brim, not scrap.
- 1-inch post rotates in either 90° or approximately 30° hole in a 2×12 base; neither rotation is locked.
- Bench/block must be raised to clear protruding grips; repeated locking/unlocking Vise-Grips proved cumbersome.
- Bambu Lab X1C available for optional snap-on jaw shoes.
