## Existing observations (reported 2026-10-08)
**Tarp clips (two owned):** approximately 5 mm jaw opening, locking collar remained fully engaged, but felt slid out. User suspects interlocking teeth with opposing orientations; likely felt marking. No measured pull load reported. **Result: reject as-is.**

**Vise-Grip contact liners:** no reported tests yet; material and geometry selection open.

## Proposed baseline protocol — not yet executed
1. Use offcut/sample of same medium-weight hat felt; document thickness and nap, dry and after steam.
2. Temporarily attach ~1.6 mm flat smooth gum rubber liners to the existing wide flat Vise-Grip faces; round exposed pad edges.
3. Clamp at low adjustable pressure, gently hand-pull in intended direction. Confirm pliers stay locked, liner does not shear/peel, and jaws maintain contact.
4. If available, increment load using small handheld scale and record when slip starts; **do not set an unverified high-force target**.
5. Release, inspect nap, pressure edges and discoloration, then inspect again after drying.
6. Repeat with similarly sized nonmarking neoprene pads, noting same conditions.
7. Record findings below, including photos or attachments as available.
