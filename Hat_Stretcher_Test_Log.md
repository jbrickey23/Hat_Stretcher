# Hat_Stretcher — test log

_Last reconciled: 2026-10-21; all findings below are user-reported unless indicated._

## Tests / field observations

### Tarp clips (2026-10-08)
- Two heavy-duty tarp clips, ~5 mm mouth.
- Felt slid out even when locking collar stayed engaged.
- Interlocking opposing tooth directions suspected of reducing resistance; potential marking concern.
- **Result: rejected as-is.** No measured load.

### Vise-Grips with 1/8-inch flat wood inserts (2026-10-08/09)
- Adequate grip to stretch felt.
- Slight compression that appeared removable by steaming and brushing.
- Wood was a proof-of-concept, **not** the preferred final material.

### Vise-Grips with flat PLA inserts (2026-10-09)
- Sufficient grip to pull/stretch felt; no reported grip slippage.
- Slight marking reportedly removed.
- **Important limitation:** most clamp pressure concentrated at leading/nose edge; broader and more uniform pressure distribution still needed if this tool remains in use.
- PLA-to-jaw retention/attachment geometry still being explored.
- A reshaped hat body already had a tear at break; force propagated it along sweatband stitching/perforation line. Failure is **not evidence of a gripper-created initial tear**. No safe load value established.

### MyLifeUNIT canvas stretching pliers (2026-10-09)
- Purchased and tried. User: 'worked great,' enough grip to pull felt, no observed marring.
- Could apply sufficient pull to tear material if excessive force used.
- Initial grip test successful; **full blocking session, fatigue, repeated use, clearance and long-term finish effects not yet evaluated**.
- **Current lead gripper**, no modification yet.
